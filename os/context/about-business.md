# About the Business

**Offer:** Robinexis builds an AI receptionist that answers missed calls 24/7, reschedules cancelled appointments, contacts waitlists to fill calendar gaps, and connects seamlessly to existing booking calendars (Google Calendar, Outlook, Fresha).

**ICP:** Appointment-based businesses — hair and beauty salons, dental clinics, barbershops, health practices — that lose revenue whenever calls go unanswered or appointments cancel.

**Revenue model:** Client subscription plans + setup fees, billed and collected through Stripe. Sales pipeline and deal values tracked in GoHighLevel (GHL) CRM. Accounting and revenue reporting via Stripe Dashboard and QuickBooks.
