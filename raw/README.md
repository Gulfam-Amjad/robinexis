# raw/ — the front door

**Drop anything here.** Articles, transcripts, PDFs, screenshots, voice notes, half-formed ideas — anything you want your AIOS to deal with. You don't sort it, you don't pick a brain, you don't name a folder. You just drop.

Then say **"process my raw"** (or `/ingest`) and the AIOS will:

1. **Read** each item and work out which brain it belongs to via `BRAINS.md`.
2. **File** it into that brain's `raw/` and **distil** the knowledge into that brain's `wiki/`.
3. **Log** it in the brain's `log.md` and clear it out of here.

`raw/` should be **empty most of the time** — it's an in-tray, not storage. If something's sitting here, it hasn't been processed yet.

---

*One vocabulary, every level: `raw/` = unsorted in · `wiki/` = distilled knowledge · `outputs/` = finished work. At the top level only `raw/` exists, because this level is the switchboard that routes into the brains — it isn't a brain itself.*
