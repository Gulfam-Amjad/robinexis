# Robinexis — a Sturdy Ai Brain

This folder is a **brain**: a Karpathy-style LLM wiki the AIOS maintains. See `brains/_template/README.md` for the full conventions.

## What kind of brain is this?

- **Name:** Robinexis
- **Type:** company
- **Scope (what belongs here):** Everything about Robinexis the business — prospects, demos built, deals and their status, voice-agent craft learned along the way, playbook knowledge.
- **Out of scope (never write here):** Anything personal/private — that lives in `brains/personal/`.

## Front-loaded at delivery (2026-07-03, by Sturdy Ai)

- `raw/Playbook_Robinexis_Voice_Agent_System.md` — the full technical playbook handed over with the Foundation.
- `wiki/concept-demo-workflow.md` — how the demo-creation system works, distilled.
